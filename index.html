01 · About

Engineer first, with data as the lens.

I'm an electrical engineer working in data centre operations and planning at Safaricom, where I help keep Tier-certified facilities running and use data to understand how they consume energy.

My work moves between the plant room and the dashboard: maintaining UPS, generator, HVAC and power distribution systems, executing live changes with zero downtime, and building the pipelines that turn monitoring data into energy KPI reporting.

I hold a Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Moi University and have completed ALX's Data Science programme. The question I keep coming back to: how do we use data to make energy-intensive infrastructure more efficient and reliable?
